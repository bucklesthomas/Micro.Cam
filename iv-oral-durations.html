IV-to-oral switch and durations

Check whether a patient meets the national criteria for switching from IV to oral antimicrobials, and look up evidence-based treatment durations.

Durations assume clinical response and source control, in adults who are not severely immunosuppressed. Follow local guidelines, and extend or shorten for the individual patient.
