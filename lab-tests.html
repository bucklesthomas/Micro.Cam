Lab test finder

Which sample to take, how to collect and send it, and which laboratory does the test, including the national reference laboratories.

Tube colours, routing and turnaround times differ between laboratories: check the local pathology handbook, and phone the lab for anything urgent or unusual.

Sources: UK Standards for Microbiology Investigations (UKHSA); UKHSA reference laboratory user manuals (RIPL, Malaria Reference Laboratory, Parasitology Reference Laboratory, Mycology Reference Laboratory, AMRHAI, GBRU, RVPBRU, Virus Reference Department); NICE NG33; BASHH guidelines.
