Gram-negative bacteraemia

Empirical choice, then targeted treatment for ESBL, AmpC, carbapenemase producers and non-fermenters, with duration and oral switch. Tap any earlier answer to change it.

A guide only, for adults. Follow your local antimicrobial policy and discuss resistant organisms with microbiology.

Sources: IDSA 2024 guidance on antimicrobial-resistant Gram-negative infections; MERINO (JAMA 2018); BALANCE (NEJM 2025); UKHSA IV-to-oral switch criteria.
