Contact prophylaxis

Who needs antibiotics, vaccine or immunoglobulin after exposure to a notifiable infection, based on current UKHSA guidance.

The local health protection team leads contact tracing and supplies immunoglobulin: always discuss with them. Doses are for guidance; check the BNF.

Sources: UKHSA guidance: meningococcal disease (2025); iGAS contacts (2023); varicella PEP (2026); measles (2026); pertussis (2026); hepatitis A (2024); Hib (2013).
