Fungal markers

How to read galactomannan, beta-D-glucan, Aspergillus PCR, cryptococcal antigen and other fungal tests, including cut-offs, pitfalls and how to use them together.

Cut-offs depend on the assay: check your laboratory’s report. Biomarkers support but do not replace imaging, culture and histology, and are interpreted with the host and clinical picture (see the EORTC/MSGERC criteria on the Scores page).

Sources: Donnelly JP et al. EORTC/MSGERC consensus definitions (Clin Infect Dis 2020); MFT Mycology: Aspergillus galactomannan and beta-D-glucan user guides; ESCMID-ECMM-ERS Aspergillus guideline (2017); ECIL-5 Pneumocystis guidelines (2016); ECMM/ISHAM/ASM cryptococcosis guideline (2024); ISHAM ABPA working group (2024); global mucormycosis guideline (Cornely OA et al. Lancet Infect Dis 2019).
