Candidaemia treatment

Answer two questions to reach a suggested treatment for adults with Candida in a blood culture. Tap any earlier answer to change it.

A guide only. Discuss every case with microbiology. Candida in sputum or urine is usually colonisation and does not need treatment.

Sources: ECMM/ISHAM/ASM global guideline for candidiasis (Lancet Infect Dis 2025); IDSA candidiasis guideline (2016); UKHSA C. auris guidance (2025).
