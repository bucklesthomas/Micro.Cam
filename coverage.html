Coverage checker

Pick the antibiotics the patient is on and the organisms you are worried about, to see whether the current regimen is likely to cover them.

Based on typical UK susceptibility, not the patient’s isolate. “Likely covered” is not a guarantee: always check culture results, the site of infection, dosing and local resistance rates.

Uses the same spectrum data as the antibiotics page, checked against the EUCAST intrinsic resistance table.
