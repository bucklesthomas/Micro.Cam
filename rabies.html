Rabies post-exposure advice

Work through the UKHSA risk assessment for a possible rabies exposure: animal, country, contact and immune status give the composite risk and the treatment. Tap any earlier answer to change it.

A guide only, for England. UKHSA’s Rabies and Immunoglobulin Service (RIgS) completes the risk assessment and supplies vaccine and immunoglobulin.

RIgS: 0330 128 1020 (9:30am–5pm, 7 days). Out of hours: highest-risk exposures only. Check the country on the UKHSA rabies risks by country list.

Sources: UKHSA rabies post-exposure treatment: management guidelines (2023), including the risk assessment summary (updated June 2025) and the interim HRIG recommendations (June 2025); RIgS.
