Exclusion and clearance

Who needs to stay off work, school or nursery, for how long, and which clearance samples are needed: cases and contacts of gastrointestinal infections in the UKHSA risk groups (food handlers, healthcare and care staff, young children in childcare), and exclusion periods for schools and nurseries.

A guide to UKHSA guidance for England. For notifiable infections the local health protection team makes the decision: discuss with them.

Exclusion periods for children and staff in children and young people’s settings. Where a gastrointestinal infection needs clearance for risk groups, use the guide above.
