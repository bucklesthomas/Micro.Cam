Penicillin allergy and cross-reactivity

Work out what kind of reaction the label means, which β-lactams can be used, and when to de-label.

About 1 in 10 people in the UK have a penicillin allergy label, and over 90% of them are not allergic. A guide only: follow your local allergy and de-labelling policy.
