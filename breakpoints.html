EUCAST breakpoint interpreter

Pick the organism and antimicrobial, then enter the MIC or disc zone diameter to see what it means under EUCAST.

EUCAST Clinical Breakpoint Tables v16.1 (2026) and Antifungal Breakpoint Table v12.1. Your laboratory’s report is the authority: check it uses the same version, method and disc content, and look out for areas of technical uncertainty (ATU), which are not shown here.

Breakpoints and notes: EUCAST Clinical Breakpoint Tables v16.1 and Antifungal Clinical Breakpoints v12.1, as encoded in the open-source AMR package. Organisms without their own EUCAST entry (e.g. K. pneumoniae) use the genus or group table. S, I and R follow the 2019 EUCAST definitions; see the EUCAST dosing table for the standard and high doses behind S and I.
