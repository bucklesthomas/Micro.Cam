Organisms

Key facts and first-line treatment for common organisms, with their antibiotic activity, intrinsic resistance and links to the relevant guides.

A quick reference for adults. Treatment depends on the site, severity and the isolate’s susceptibility: follow local guidelines and discuss with microbiology.

Treatment summaries draw on the guides on this site and their sources (IDSA 2024 AMR guidance, ESC 2023, UK meningitis 2016, UKHSA, ECMM/ISHAM), with BASHH for gonorrhoea and NICE for common infections. Intrinsic resistance: EUCAST expected phenotypes v1.2. AmpC risk groups: IDSA 2024.
