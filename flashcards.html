CICE flashcards

Revision cards for the Combined Infection Certificate Examination: bacteriology, virology, mycology and parasitology, antimicrobials, infection prevention, clinical infection and lab methods, plus decks built from this site’s data.

For revision only. Answers reflect UK guidance current when written; always check the source guideline. Pick one or more decks, tap a card to reveal the answer, and mark it “Got it” or “Again”. Progress is saved in this browser only. Keyboard: select a card, then Space to flip and ← → to move.

Topics follow the RCPath CICE areas. Content draws on the guides on this site and their sources (EUCAST, UKHSA, BHIVA, NICE, BSG, ESC, IDSA, EORTC/MSGERC, UK SMIs, the Green Book).
