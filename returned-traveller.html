Returned traveller with fever

HCID screen first, then the likely causes by region and incubation period, first-line tests and empirical treatment.

A guide only. Exclude malaria first in anyone who has been to a malaria area, and use the ACDP VHF algorithm where relevant.

Differential finder

Tap the region, onset, symptoms, exposures and blood results you have. The list re-ranks as you go and shows what matched and what to send.

Sources: CDC Yellow Book, post-travel evaluation (incubation periods and clinical clues); GeoSentinel surveillance; Johnston V et al. Fever in returned travellers presenting in the United Kingdom (J Infect 2009); ACDP VHF algorithm; UK malaria guideline (2016); TravelHealthPro for current outbreaks.
