Vancomycin AUC calculator

AUC24 from a peak and trough (intermittent dosing) or a steady-state level (continuous infusion), with half-life, Vd and clearance, and regimens predicted to reach your target.

Adults with stable renal function only. Not for haemodialysis, CRRT, ECMO, rapidly changing renal function, children or pregnancy. Follow your trust's vancomycin protocol and check with pharmacy before changing a dose.

Notes

  • Target AUC24 400–600 mg·h/L for serious MRSA infection, assuming a vancomycin MIC of 1 mg/L (2020 ASHP/IDSA/PIDS/SIDP consensus). Above 600, kidney injury becomes more likely.
  • Two-level method: take both levels in the same dosing interval, ideally at steady state (around the 4th dose): a peak 1–2 hours after the end of the infusion, and a trough within 1 hour before the next dose.
  • Record the exact infusion start, end and sample times: small timing errors change the AUC noticeably.
  • A trough alone underestimates or overestimates the AUC in many patients; Bayesian software (where your trust has it) can estimate the AUC from a single level.
  • Recheck levels after any dose change, and at least weekly (more often with changing renal function, nephrotoxins or critical illness).

Sources: Rybak MJ et al. Therapeutic monitoring of vancomycin for serious MRSA infections: a revised consensus guideline (Am J Health Syst Pharm 2020); Pai MP et al. Innovative approaches to optimizing the delivery of vancomycin in individual patients (Adv Drug Deliv Rev 2014); Sawchuk RJ, Zaske DE (J Pharmacokinet Biopharm 1976); BNF vancomycin (maximum infusion rate 10 mg/min).
