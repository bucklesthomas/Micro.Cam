Intrinsic resistance

Antibiotics each organism is expected to be resistant to, whatever the laboratory result. Look up by organism, or by antibiotic to see which organisms it will not cover.

Only intrinsic (expected) resistance is shown. An antibiotic not listed is not necessarily active: acquired resistance is common, so always use the isolate’s susceptibility results. AmpC high risk of clinically significant chromosomal AmpC; AmpC AmpC present but low risk (IDSA 2024).

Source: EUCAST Expected Resistant Phenotypes, version 1.2 (2023), as encoded in the open-source AMR package, with notes from the EUCAST footnotes. AmpC risk groups from the IDSA 2024 guidance on antimicrobial-resistant Gram-negative infections.
