Antimicrobials in pregnancy and breastfeeding

A quick guide to which antibacterials, antifungals, antivirals and antimalarials can be used in pregnancy and while breastfeeding.

A summary only. Treat serious infection properly: the risk of untreated infection usually outweighs the drug risk. Check the UKTIS monograph (bumps) and the SPS breastfeeding guidance for the individual drug, and involve obstetrics and pharmacy.

Generally compatibleUse with careAvoid

Sources: UKTIS: Medicines in Pregnancy (bumps); SPS: safety in breastfeeding; BNF; MHRA Drug Safety Update (fluconazole, 2019); UK malaria treatment guideline (2016) and WHO malaria guidelines; BHIVA pregnancy guidelines.
