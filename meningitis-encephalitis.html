Meningitis and encephalitis

Empirical treatment, LP and CT decisions, dexamethasone and aciclovir, then targeted treatment once the organism is known.

A guide only, for adults. Treatment must not be delayed: give antibiotics within 1 hour. Discuss with infection and neurology teams.

Sources: UK joint specialist societies meningitis guideline (2016); UK encephalitis guideline (2026); BIA TB meningitis guideline (2009).
