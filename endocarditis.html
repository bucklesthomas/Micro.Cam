Endocarditis therapy

Answer each question to reach a suggested antibiotic regimen for adults, based on the 2023 ESC guidelines. Tap any earlier answer to change it.

A guide only. Confirm every regimen with microbiology/ID and the Endocarditis Team, and adjust doses to weight, renal function and local guidelines. For diagnosis, use the modified Duke criteria.

Source: 2023 ESC Guidelines for the management of endocarditis (Delgado V et al. Eur Heart J 2023;44:3948). Regimens, doses and classes are taken from Recommendation Tables 7–10, Table 11 and Section 7.
