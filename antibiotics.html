Antibiotics

Common UK antibiotics with their UK-AWaRe category, usual adult doses, PK/PD targets and spectrum. Tap an antibiotic for details.

Usual adult doses for normal renal function, as a quick reference. Check the BNF, your local antimicrobial guidelines and the patient’s renal function, weight and interactions before prescribing.

UK-AWaRe categories: UKHSA UK-AWaRe antibiotic classification 2024 (rifampicin is not classified). Doses: usual adult doses from the BNF and UK product information. PK/PD targets and parameters from product information and standard pharmacology references; spectrum is a general guide to typical UK isolates, so always use local susceptibility data. Intrinsic resistance: EUCAST expected phenotypes.
